IES Jaroso will once again take part in an Erasmus project for higher vocational education. We have been awarded a KA131 project for the 2026–2028 period.

The final decision was published yesterday, and for this period we have four mobility places available for students and one training mobility for a member of staff.

So, we’re setting off on our Erasmus adventure once again. Let’s go!
